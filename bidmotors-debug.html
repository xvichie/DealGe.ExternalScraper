Nowadays, more and more used cars from the USA and Canada are being imported into Central and Eastern European countries.

1. Affordable Transport Costs

According to a 2019 IHS Markit study, the average American changes their car every 6.7 years. This leads to a higher level of competition and consequently lower car prices.

2. Good Vehicle Condition

With thorough preliminary research of the car’s history, you can acquire a vehicle with excellent operational characteristics that are on par with brand new models.

3. Wide Range of Models

You can easily choose a stylish business-class sedan or a practical crossover for city or off-road driving.

4. Low Mileage

If you are looking only for low mileage models, you will have a wide selection of cars available.

5. Fast Delivery

You can receive your new car within a few months. Delivery is carried out by cargo ships departing from the east coast of the United States or Canada. Delivery times may vary depending on the location of the starting and destination points.

Purchasing a vehicle from the USA and Canada is done through specialized services operating on an auction principle, with two main types of bidding:

  1. Pre-Bidding In this option, potential buyers leave the price they are willing to pay for the vehicle. The highest bid wins. Important: the car is still offered at an online auction afterward.
  2. Online Bidding The auction takes place in real time, with buyers bidding against each other. Important: if the pre-bid exceeds the highest bid from the online auction, the pre-bid is considered the winning bid

If during the auction no buyer offers the seller’s minimum price, the car returns to the pre-auction.

Before bidding on a specific car, carefully research its history. The attached photos may not reflect the actual technical condition of the vehicle. To obtain objective information about its history, consult specialized services that can provide records for the cars you are interested in. These records include all data on previous accidents and servicing.

Delivery and customs clearance are important steps in the process. On average, the delivery of such cars to Bulgaria takes from 30 to 60 days. This period includes the transportation of cars from auctions (Copart or IAAI) to the nearest ports, where the loading of transport ships to Europe takes place.

Customs clearance is a process involving the calculation of excise duty (based on the technical characteristics of the internal combustion engine and the year of manufacture of the car), import duties and VAT.
